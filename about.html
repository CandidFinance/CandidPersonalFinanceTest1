About Candid

Candid works out what your money could be doing, in pounds, from your own figures: pension and salary sacrifice, ISAs and investments, cash savings, student loans, mortgages and debt. It checks your setup against UK tax rules and allowances and shows what each option is worth, so you can compare them. The decisions are yours.

Candid is guidance, not regulated financial advice. What Candid is and isn't explains what that means in practice.
