# Candid synthetic personas (50)

Synthetic personas for a structured UX stress test of Candid. They are invented, not real users, and every reaction recorded against them is simulated inference. Validate the top findings with 5–10 real people from the target segment.

**Spread:** 31 mobile / 19 desktop. 7 Scottish taxpayers. 11 likely to bounce within a minute (marked **[bounce risk]**). 9 poor fits for the product (marked **[poor fit]**). Arrival: TikTok 15, friend referral 10, search 9, LinkedIn 7, Substack 6, AI assistant 3.

Fields in each entry: **job and income** · **location** · **household** · **literacy** · **products and habits** · **worries** · **motivation** · **trust** · **arrival** · **device** · **time**.
